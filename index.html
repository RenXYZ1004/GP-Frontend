Process

How It Works

Three simple steps to get your official permanent gatepass reviewed and approved.

01

Submit Your Application

Fill out the online form with your student details and guardian contact information. It only takes a minute.

02

Admin Review

The school admin reviews your application. You will be notified by email once it has been reviewed and a decision is made.

03

Receive Your Gatepass

Once approved, your digital gatepass with a unique QR code is issued — ready to use at all school gate checkpoints.
