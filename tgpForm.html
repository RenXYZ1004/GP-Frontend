About Temporary Gate Passes
  • A TGP is a one-day pass for students who need to exit the school on a specific date.
  • After submitting, expect a call from the school at the contact number you provide to confirm your request.
  • Once approved by the school secretary, the pass will be emailed to the student's email address.
  • Do not proceed to the gate until you receive the approved pass by email.
